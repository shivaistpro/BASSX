INSTALLAZIONE BASSX

INSTALLAZIONE RAPIDA
  Windows: doppio click su "Install BASSX.bat" (chiede i permessi di amministratore).
  macOS:   doppio click su "Install BASSX.command" (se bloccato: tasto destro > Apri).
  Oppure segui i passi manuali qui sotto.


macOS
1. Copia BASSX.component in  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   (oppure ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components)
2. Copia BASSX.vst3 in        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   (oppure ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3)
3. (Opzionale) Copia l'applicazione BASSX.app in Applicazioni o sulla Scrivania
4. Apri Ableton Live e riscansiona i plug-in

Windows
1. Copia la cartella BASSX.vst3 in  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
2. (Opzionale) Copia BASSX.exe (versione standalone) dove preferisci
3. Apri Ableton Live e riscansiona i plug-in

Ext Mode (pagina FISH): in Ableton Live attiva l'ingresso sidechain di BASSX
(menu del dispositivo) e scegli la traccia audio da processare.

I preset utente vengono salvati in:
  macOS:   ~/Library/BASSX/Presets
  Windows: %APPDATA%\BASSX\Presets



BASSX INSTALLATION

QUICK INSTALL
  Windows: double-click "Install BASSX.bat" (asks for administrator rights).
  macOS:   double-click "Install BASSX.command" (if blocked: right-click > Open).
  Or follow the manual steps below.


macOS
1. Copy BASSX.component to  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
   (or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components)
2. Copy BASSX.vst3 to        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   (or ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3)
3. (Optional) Copy the BASSX.app application to Applications or your Desktop
4. Open Ableton Live and rescan your plug-ins

Windows
1. Copy the BASSX.vst3 folder to  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
2. (Optional) Copy BASSX.exe (standalone version) wherever you like
3. Open Ableton Live and rescan your plug-ins

Ext Mode (FISH page): in Ableton Live enable BASSX's sidechain input
(device menu) and pick the audio track to process.

User presets are saved in:
  macOS:   ~/Library/BASSX/Presets
  Windows: %APPDATA%\BASSX\Presets
